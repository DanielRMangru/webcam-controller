Connect to Zoom, MS Teams & Browsers

When Virtual Camera is active, your zoomed, framed, and auto-tracked video stream is sent directly to a virtual camera device in Linux.

Virtual Device Node

/dev/video10
One-Time Setup Command (if loopback not loaded):
sudo modprobe v4l2loopback devices=1 video_nr=10 card_label="Webcam Controller Virtual Cam" exclusive_caps=1

How to select in your apps:

  1. Open MS Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, or Chrome.
  2. Go to Settings → Video / Camera.
  3. Select "Webcam Controller Virtual Cam" as your camera device.
